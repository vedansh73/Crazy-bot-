Get started in two minutes

Add the bot

Choose Add to Discord and pick your server. You need the Manage Server permission.

Raise the bot's role

In Server Settings, open Roles and place the bot's role above your members' roles so timeouts work.

Start chatting

Write in any channel and the bot replies. Moderation runs automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Does the bot ban members?

No. Automatic actions are timeouts only (mutes on Telegram). Kick and ban are manual commands that only the owner can run.

Who can run admin commands?

Moderation and settings commands are limited to the bot owner. Automatic moderation works in every server where the bot is added.

What data does the bot keep?

It keeps short per-member conversation summaries and moderation records so it can remember context and track warnings. Members can erase their own memory with !forget on Discord or /forget on Telegram.

Is it free to use?

Adding the bot is free. AI replies depend on the available daily quota, so they may pause when the limit is reached.
